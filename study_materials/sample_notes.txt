Newton's Laws of Motion & Basic Mechanics Notes

1. First Law (Inertia):
An object remains at rest or in uniform straight-line motion unless acted upon by a net external force.

2. Second Law (Force and Acceleration):
Force equals mass times acceleration (F = m * a).
If mass m = 5 kg and acceleration a = 3 m/s^2, the force is F = 5 * 3 = 15 Newtons.

3. Third Law (Action and Reaction):
For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Key Energy Formulas:
- Kinetic Energy: KE = 0.5 * m * v^2
- Gravitational Potential Energy: PE = m * g * h (using g = 9.8 m/s^2)
- Work Done: W = F * d (in Joules)
